User Guide / Manual

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Job Portal System. This guide will help you understand how to use all features of the platform, whether you're a jobseeker or an employer.

2. Getting Started

Creating an Account: Register as either a Jobseeker or an Employer using the sign-up page. Employers are required to enter company details during registration.

Logging In: Use your email and password to access your personalized dashboard.

3. For Jobseekers

  • Update Profile: Go to "Edit Profile" to add your education, work experience, and skills.
  • Search Jobs: Use the search bar on the dashboard or homepage to filter jobs by title, location, or category.
  • Apply for Jobs: Click “Apply Now” on job listings. You can upload a new resume or use the one saved in your profile.
  • Track Applications: View your submitted applications, status updates, and feedback from employers in your dashboard.

4. For Employers

  • Create Job Posts: Go to “Post a Job” to add new listings with title, description, requirements, and status.
  • Manage Jobs: View, edit, or close active job listings from your employer dashboard.
  • View Applicants: Check applicants for each job with their resumes, profiles, and application date.
  • Shortlist & Hire: Shortlist qualified candidates, invite them for interviews, or hire directly through the dashboard.

5. Tips & Best Practices

  • Keep your profile updated for better visibility.
  • Upload tailored resumes for different jobs.
  • Employers should provide detailed job descriptions to attract the right candidates.
  • Use the FAQs or Contact Form if you encounter issues.

6. Troubleshooting & Support

For help, visit the FAQs page, use the Contact Query Form, or reach out via Live Chat (if available).
